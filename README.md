## 🌍 Background

I completed my medical degree in Sudan while the country navigated four consecutive national disruptions: the 2019
revolution, COVID-19, a six-month faculty strike, and an ongoing armed conflict since April 2023. During those years I
led the university's medical students' association as Secretary General and later as President (800+ students), built
organisational software from scratch, and delivered 25 research data analysis projects for clients across Sudan while
supporting my family through displacement.

I am currently building an independent research project on AI diagnostic tools for African neuroimaging data — a gap
that is documented in the literature but almost entirely unaddressed. My background as a clinician who has worked in
Sudan's health system gives me a perspective on this problem that purely computational researchers don't have.
